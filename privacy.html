Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Overview

AI Library is a private, personal-use tool operated solely for its owner. It helps the owner organize, classify, and search visual reference material saved in the owner’s personal Pinterest account.

Information the tool may read

With the owner’s authorization, AI Library may read:

  • Pinterest Boards
  • Board Sections
  • Pins
  • Pin metadata, such as titles, descriptions, links, image references, and organizational relationships

How information is used

The information is used only to organize the owner’s content, support AI-assisted classification, and enable private search. The relevant metadata is stored locally for these purposes.

Data sharing and sales

AI Library does not sell user data. It does not share user data with third parties. The tool is not offered as a service to other users and does not manage or represent other users’ accounts.

Advertising

AI Library does not use Pinterest data for advertising, ad targeting, audience building, or ad measurement.

Data control

Because this is a personal tool, the owner controls the local data it stores and may remove that local data at any time. Access to Pinterest data depends on the owner’s authorization and may be revoked through the relevant account settings.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the tool’s functionality or data practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page.
